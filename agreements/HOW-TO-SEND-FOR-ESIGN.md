# How to send the HARZ.GG Developer Services Agreement for e-sign

**Best option for one developer agreement:** [Dropbox Sign](https://sign.dropbox.com/) (free tier: 3 signature requests/month).

## Files in this folder

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `HARZ-GG-Developer-Services-Agreement.pdf` | Upload this to Dropbox Sign / DocuSign |
| `HARZ-GG-Developer-Services-Agreement.html` | Edit text if needed, then re-export to PDF |

## Recommended flow (Dropbox Sign — free)

1. Open `HARZ-GG-Developer-Services-Agreement.pdf`.
2. Fill in the **Effective Date** (or leave it as a field for you to complete in the e-sign tool).
3. Go to [sign.dropbox.com](https://sign.dropbox.com/) and create a free account.
4. Click **Sign or send a document** → upload the PDF.
5. Add two signers:
   - **Owner:** Spencer Harz (your email)
   - **Developer:** V (their email)
6. Place signature, printed-name, and date fields on the Owner and Developer signature blocks.
7. Send. Both parties get email links. You get a completed PDF + audit trail when both finish.

## Tips

- Send from your HARZ.GG / personal business email so it looks official.
- Keep the signed PDF + Dropbox Sign completion certificate in a private folder (not in a public repo).
- Do **not** put SSH keys, passwords, or credentials in the agreement or the e-sign email.
- Replace “V” with the developer’s legal name before sending if you have it.

## Not legal advice

E-signatures are generally valid under U.S. ESIGN / UETA for this kind of contract, but enforceability of specific clauses (especially non-compete, liability, and vague compensation) depends on Kentucky law and facts. Have an attorney review if this relationship is important.
