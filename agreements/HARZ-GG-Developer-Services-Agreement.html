HARZ.GG / SD-Online Platform

Developer Services Agreement

Effective Date: ______________________

This Developer Services Agreement (“Agreement”) is made between:

Owner: HARZ.GG / Spencer Harz

Developer: V

1. Scope of Work

Developer agrees to provide software development services for the SD-Online Platform (“Platform”), including but not limited to coding, debugging, feature development, security improvements, maintenance, and any additional tasks assigned by Owner.

2. Confidentiality & Security Requirements

Developer acknowledges they will have access to sensitive and confidential information, including but not limited to:

  • Platform passwords
  • SSH keys
  • API keys
  • Server credentials
  • Internal documentation
  • Business strategies, communications, and private data

Developer agrees to the following:

  • No disclosure of any confidential information to any third party.
  • No copying, storing, screenshotting, forwarding, or reusing credentials or confidential data.
  • No sharing of access keys, passwords, or platform-related information.
  • Immediate reporting of any suspected vulnerabilities, breaches, or unauthorized access.
  • Strict adherence to all security instructions provided by Owner.

Any disclosure, mishandling, negligence, or unauthorized use of credentials or confidential information that results in — or could reasonably result in — a security risk, breach, data loss, platform downtime, or financial harm may result in:

  • Immediate termination
  • Revocation of access
  • Legal action, including civil court proceedings

3. Intellectual Property Ownership

All code, assets, designs, documentation, and work produced by Developer for HARZ.GG are the exclusive property of Owner.

Developer agrees:

  • All rights are assigned to Owner.
  • Developer may not reuse, resell, repurpose, or distribute any part of the work.
  • Developer may not claim ownership of any work created under this Agreement.

4. Compensation

Developer will receive commission percentages, bonuses, or other performance-based rewards as determined by Owner. Compensation may be adjusted as the Platform grows or project needs change.

5. Independent Contractor Status

Developer is an independent contractor, not an employee. Developer is responsible for their own taxes, equipment, and work environment.

6. Non-Competition & Non-Interference

Developer agrees not to:

  • Build or assist in building any competing platform
  • Interfere with HARZ.GG operations
  • Attempt to harm the Platform, business, or reputation

These restrictions apply during the Agreement and for 12 months after termination.

7. Termination

Owner may terminate this Agreement at any time if Developer:

  • Violates confidentiality
  • Mishandles credentials
  • Causes or risks a security breach
  • Fails to complete assigned work
  • Acts against the interests of HARZ.GG

Upon termination, Developer must immediately return all credentials, files, code, documentation, and access.

8. Liability

Developer may be held financially responsible for damages caused by:

  • Negligence
  • Credential leaks
  • Unauthorized access
  • Intentional harm
  • Security breaches resulting from Developer’s actions

9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Kentucky.
