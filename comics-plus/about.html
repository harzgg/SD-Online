About COMICS PLUS+

A static HTML template for testing a comics & games storefront on your desktop.

What this template includes

  • Home hero with brand-first COMICS PLUS+ identity
  • Full online shop with filters, search, and sort
  • Comics and games category pages
  • Product detail pages with quantity add-to-cart
  • Cart with shipping / tax totals
  • Checkout form and success confirmation
  • Color system: yellow, white, gray, black

How to test

Copy the comics-plus folder to your desktop and open index.html in any browser. The cart uses localStorage — no server needed.
